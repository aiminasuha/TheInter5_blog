02 / Our Identity

THE STORY BEHIND OUR NAME

Why “The Interface Five”?

We chose the name The Interface Five because it reflects both our team identity and the fundamental concept of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). The word “Interface” represents the connection between humans and computer systems, highlighting the importance of creating technology that is intuitive, accessible, and user-friendly. “Five” represents our five members: Aimi, Carmelia, Kamala, Khadeeja, and Emily. Each of us brings different ideas, perspectives, and strengths, but we share one goal: to design meaningful and effective user experiences. Through teamwork, creativity, and problem-solving, we aim to apply HCI principles to solutions that meet real users’ needs.
